# SOUCOFFE - Screen List & Experience Map

## Customer Journey (Mobile-First Focus)
1. **Welcome / Landing Page**: Premium hero section with "SOUCOFFE" branding, intro to QR ordering, and primary CTAs.
2. **QR/Table Identification**: Entrance screen asking for the customer's name and confirming the table number (detected from URL).
3. **Main Digital Menu**: Category-based browsing with horizontal scroll, popular items, and special offers.
4. **Category Page (Cafés)**: Focused view of coffee selections with refined cards.
5. **Product Details (Modal)**: Customization options (size, sugar, extras) and quantity selector.
6. **Smart Cart**: Overview of selected items with table/name context and pricing summary.
7. **Order Success & Tracking**: Real-time progress timeline (Received -> Preparing -> Ready -> Served).
8. **Digital Invoice / Receipt**: Clean, printable summary of the order with "New Order" action.

## Admin Experience
9. **Admin Dashboard**: Analytics overview (sales, top products, order volume).
10. **Real-time Orders**: Management board for staff to accept and update order statuses.
11. **Table Management**: Visual grid of cafeteria tables with QR codes and current status.
12. **Product Management**: Interface to add/edit menu items, prices, and availability.
